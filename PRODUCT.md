# Product

This is the product brief for AIOS. Claude reads it alongside `DESIGN.md` before design work: DESIGN.md says how things look, this file says who it is for and what it must do. Rewrite the Users section for yourself; the rest usually holds.

## Register

product (a working tool that can also be shown on stage or in a screen share)

## Users

First: one operator who runs several projects at once (clients, content, a team, side builds) with thousands of files across a laptop, external drives and cloud folders, and Claude Code sessions in all of them. Second: anyone watching that person share their screen, on a call or a stream, on laptops and phones over compressed video.

## Product purpose

AIOS turns every project, skill, agent and routine into one calm operating system: what exists, what moved today, what the agents are doing, and how the pieces feed each other (the Brain). Success means a viewer can understand the whole operation at a glance, and the owner can find anything in two clicks.

## Brand personality

Clean, confident, quietly premium. White ground, navy ink, one cyan signal, with a liquid glass layer. Builder-led and specific, never hype.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS dashboards, purple-blue gradient AI slop, neon or colored glows, dark "hacker" control rooms, fully rounded pill buttons everywhere, wide uppercase tracking, nested cards, identical card grids, hero-metric templates, fake terminals, decorative effects that compete with the content.

## Design principles

1. The work is the hero: real file names, real counts, real connections.
2. Glass is a material, not a gimmick: panels float over a living but quiet ground.
3. Cyan means "this matters": the accent is reserved for live state, selection, primary actions and connections.
4. Legible on a stream: generous type, strong contrast, one idea per region.
5. Motion shows life: things reveal, the feed ticks, the Brain flows.

## Accessibility

WCAG AA text contrast; reduced-motion users get static states; keyboard navigation (⌘K, 1-9, Esc) throughout.
